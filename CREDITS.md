# Credits

This dataset is compiled from public information published by the people and organisations below. Route facts (numbers, termini, stop order, timings, fares) were extracted and normalised; full source details, fetch dates and licence notes are in [sources/SOURCES.md](sources/SOURCES.md).

## Maps and coordinates

- **OpenStreetMap contributors**: stop and station coordinates, route checks, ferry crossings. © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Geocoding via [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/) and the [Overpass API](https://overpass-api.de/). Regional extract from [Geofabrik](https://download.geofabrik.de/).

## Bus and minibus

- **West Bengal Transport Corporation (WBTC)**: [city bus route list](https://wbtconline.in/wbtc-city-bus-routes).
- **Kolkata Bus-O-Pedia Foundation**: [private](https://www.kolbusopedia.com/bus-routes) and [government](https://www.kolbusopedia.com/bus-routes-government) route catalogues, the backbone of the bus layer. Thank you.
- **BusBondhu** by Rounak Adhikary: consulted to locate the upstream sources above. No BusBondhu data is included.

## Metro, suburban rail, ferry and tram

- **Metro Railway, Kolkata**: [official timetables](https://mtp.indianrailways.gov.in/).
- **erail.in**: suburban train timetables and stop lists.
- **West Bengal Transport Department** and **WBTC**: ferry routes and fares, tram notes.
- **Wikipedia contributors** ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)): Kolkata Metro, Kolkata Suburban Railway, Trams in Kolkata.
- **Calcutta Tramways Company**, **Traveling Creature**, **Kolkata Dekho**, **Kolkata On Wheels**, **KolkataOnline**: ferry and tram service details.

## Autos and fares

- **Kolkata City Info** and **F3Kolkata** (via the [Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/)): auto routes and stops.
- **Bartaman**, **Aaj Tak Bangla**, **Ei Muhurte**, **Subkuz**, **Banglahunt**, **TV9 Bangla**: 2026 auto fares.
- **wbxpress.com**: West Bengal bus, taxi and auto-rickshaw notifications.
- **ThePrint**, **The Statesman**, **The Week**, **NewKerala**, **Business Standard**: fare and service reporting.
- **Train Help**, **kolkatametrorail.com**, **kolkatametro.org**, **metrofare.in**, **yometro.com**: fare and pass cross-checks.

## Tools

- [MobilityData GTFS Validator](https://github.com/MobilityData/gtfs-validator) for feed validation.

If you own one of these sources and want the credit changed or your data removed, open an issue.
