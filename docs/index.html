Kolkata Trip Planner POC

Bus, minibus, metro, local train, share auto, ferry and tram, routed in your browser.

      Type to pick a stop. Press Enter or choose "Search places" to look up an address. You can also tap the map.

      Modes

      Walking is always included for getting to stops and changing.

      Read before you travel

      • Bus, minibus and share auto frequencies and times are estimates: no source publishes them, so buses assume a bus every 15 min and autos every 10 min, at about 15 to 18 km/h.
      • Metro and train run times come mostly from published timetables (rail times from community timetable sites); ferry and tram service is community-sourced. Waits are averaged from frequency, not live departures, and the planner ignores time of day: many routes stop in the evening.
      • This is a community dataset with gaps: about a quarter of bus stops have no coordinates yet and are missing.
