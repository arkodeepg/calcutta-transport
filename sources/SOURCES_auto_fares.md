## Notes added 2026-10-05

- Official coverage: the 489 routes of AF-RTA5673 span Howrah (serials 1 to 18), south and south-west Kolkata, Behala, Garia, Jadavpur, Tollygunge (about 19 to 82 and 160 to 296), Salt Lake, Rajarhat, New Town and Airport (83 to 159), and Dum Dum, Barrackpore, Baranagar, Dakshineswar, Sodepur, Titagarh (297 to 489). Alipore, Ballygunge, Park Circus and Central Kolkata routes sit mostly in 160 to 296.
- Seventeen existing hand-built routes match an official serial exactly and are marked verified with the serial in the notes; their stop lists remain community data.
- Serials 53 and 54 each list three alternative destinations and are split into three routes each (suffix a, b, c).
- No official or news source gives per-route auto fares for these routes beyond the March 2026 reports already listed. The 2017 Amar Kolkata Auto fare ranges are app estimates and are not used.
- Searched on 2026-10-05 without finding further route lists: Reddit r/kolkata and Quora (no relevant threads surfaced), Wikimapia (only individual stand pages such as Dum Dum - Nagerbazar auto stand; its user-contributed coordinates are not clearly ODbL compatible, so not used), the Tripadvisor forum and shareautos.in (already AF-CI and AF-SA), and a 2011 blog kolkataauto.blogspot.com (seven Jadavpur and Gariahat corridors, no numbers or fares; not loaded because those corridors are covered by official serials). Kolkata Police traffic notices, NKDA and HIDCO e-rickshaw or toto lists and Bartaman, Sangbad Pratidin, Anandabazar and Telegraph route-by-route lists were searched in English and Bengali; only the March 2026 fare stories already recorded came up.
