# Sources: bus and minibus

Build: `venv/bin/python scripts/bus_fetch_upstream.py`, `scripts/bus_fetch_osm.py`, `scripts/bus_fetch_osm.py --pbf sources/raw/bus/eastern-zone-latest.osm.pbf` (OSM gazetteer from the Geofabrik extract), `scripts/bus_fetch_gazetteers.py` (Wikidata, GeoNames), then `scripts/bus_build.py`, then `scripts/bus_geocode_nominatim.py`, then `scripts/bus_build.py` again. Raw inputs live in `sources/raw/bus/` (git-ignored): the upstream pages are cached under `sources/raw/bus/upstream/` with `fetch_log.json` (URL, UTC time, HTTP status). The build log with merge, rejection and split decisions is `sources/raw/bus/build_report.txt`.

Licence position: WBTC and Bus-O-Pedia state no open licence ("All Rights Reserved" footers). Only facts (route numbers, termini, stop names and order) are extracted and republished with credit; their pages are not redistributed. This is a facts-only position, not permission; India recognises copyright in compilations, so if either owner objects the affected rows will be removed.

Fetch policy: at most 1 request per 2 seconds to the route-list sites, 1 per second to Nominatim, one Wikidata SPARQL query at a time with a 2 s pause (63 tiles), one download each for Geofabrik and GeoNames, a descriptive User-Agent with no personal details, everything cached so a rebuild is offline.

## Notes and gaps

- No published source carries headways, first/last service or route-level fares for these routes, so those columns are blank. Fare slabs (WBTC/private/minibus) belong to the fares dataset.
- `confidence`: verified = in WBTC's own published list; community = Bus-O-Pedia or OSM only.
- When a government route number is published by both WBTC and Bus-O-Pedia, the list that names more stops is kept and the other list's termini are recorded in `notes`. A private route that shares a number with a WBTC route is kept as a separate route (suffix `_2`).
- Stop identity: spellings are grouped by a normalised key (case, punctuation, common Bengali-English transliteration variants, abbreviations such as Stn, Xing, Rd). Other spellings are listed in each stop's `notes`. One name used for places far apart is split into separate ids.
- Direction 1 in `route_stops.csv` is direction 0 reversed unless OSM supplied a second-direction relation. Kolkata routes often use one-way streets on the return, so the reverse order is approximate. Where a source printed the stoppages from the far end, the list is reordered and the route `notes` say so.
- `travel_min_from_start` is blank: neither source publishes run times.
- OSM-only routes: stops are ordered along the relation's way chain when every stop lies within 300 m of it, because some relations list their stop members out of travel order (r2900668, L30B, listed Babu Ghat last although its ways start at the Esplanade end). The route `notes` say when this reordering was applied. The Overpass extract of that relation carries only 4 named stop members, so stops mapped only on the ground (Shyambazar, Dum Dum and others) are not in the data.
- Neither upstream list publishes coordinates. Every coordinate comes from OpenStreetMap (Overpass, the Geofabrik extract, or Nominatim), Wikidata or GeoNames, never from a commercial geocoder, and passed a route-geometry check on every route that lists the stop: distance to the nearest located neighbour on each side within 1.5 km + 3 x stop gap x local spacing (taken from the located pair around the point, 1 to 10 km per stop, capped at twice the route's median); no detour over 1.6x + 3 km between the two neighbours; no fold-back at either neighbour (1.3x + 2 km, or a U-turn over 120 degrees, against the located stop at least 3 km beyond it); and within 1 km of the OSM route line where OSM has the same route number. A point is accepted on a clear majority of those tests (a tie only if no failing test is a shape failure). All points are re-checked leave-one-out, worst offender first; a point that only one route can test is rejected if it fails that test. Rejected points are listed in each stop's `notes`.
- Homonyms: when a point fits some routes but another route's own well-placed neighbours put that stop 15 km or more away, or it forms an out-and-back spike there, that route gets its own stop id with the same name (`_2`, `_3` suffix; e.g. a second Karunamoyee near Tollygunge, a second Bishnupur near Rajarhat, Bengal Chemical at Panihati). The new id is then located on its own or left blank.
- Seeds: a stop no neighbour can test yet is seeded only when its exact name matches a single OSM feature cluster. A stop whose name (or alias) matches the only OSM town or city of that name in the region is placed at that town and, because long-distance lists name only towns 30 to 70 km apart, its distance limits are waived while the detour and fold-back tests still apply. A far terminus that no route can test closer than 3 published stops is left blank when several places share its name.
- `coord_method` and `coord_confidence` columns in `stops.csv`: method as above (stop_variant = coordinate of another located spelling of the same stop, matched by consonant skeleton); confidence high = OSM-named point passing 2+ route tests (or on the same-number OSM relation), medium = 1 passing test or a fuzzy, Bengali, GeoNames or variant match.
- Optional, off by default: `bus_build.py --interpolate` places an unlocated stop on the same-number OSM route line between located neighbours at most 3 published stops and 3 km apart (76 stops; coverage 76.3%), as coord_method=interpolated, coord_confidence=low. It is not used in the published CSVs because `gtfs/stops.txt` cannot carry the flag, so a feed consumer could not tell an estimated point from a mapped one.
- Still blank (1185 stops, 3059 of 11844 direction-0 route stops): 706 appear on one route only; 144 had a candidate that failed the route check; 36 are split homonym ids. Many central stops (Moulali, Chandni Market, Khanna Cinema, Tollygunge Phari, 4 No. Bridge, Bandha Ghat, Mallick Fatak, Salkia Chowrasta) have no matching name in OSM, Wikidata or GeoNames as of this fetch. Adding those names to OpenStreetMap is the open way to fill them.
