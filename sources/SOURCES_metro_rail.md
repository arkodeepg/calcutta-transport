# Sources: metro, suburban rail, ferry, tram

Every `source_id` used in `data/metro`, `data/rail`, `data/ferry` and `data/tram` refers to a row below.
Raw downloads are in `sources/raw/` (paths in the coverage column; not committed). Builder scripts are in `scripts/`.

Licence column: where a source states no open licence, only facts (names, order, times, fares) were extracted and are republished with attribution under the project's ODbL; no source text, tables, PDFs or pages are redistributed. That is a facts-only position, not a permission from the source; if a source owner objects, open an issue.

Per-day metro service windows used by the GTFS build are in `data/metro/service_periods.csv` (from S-MR-03, S-MR-11, S-MR-12). Cross-mode interchanges that name matching misses are in `data/interchanges.csv` (stop pairs and distances from S-MR-01 coordinates).
